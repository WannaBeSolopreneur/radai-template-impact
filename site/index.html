    How it's built

    Goal status and the action list are columns in the warehouse, not text typed into the page.

    • A pace goal is on track when the value is at least 95% of a straight-line pace, at risk from 80% up to that line, and behind below 80%. Day 33 of a 92-day quarter is the line used here.
    • A snapshot goal uses those same cutoffs against the full target. Coverage, retention, adoption, and minutes saved are snapshot goals.
    • Bookings are closed-won ARR since October 1. New logos are first-time customers in that set.
    • Pipeline coverage is open pipeline from the meeting stage on, divided by bookings still left in the quarter.
    • Marketing pipeline counts meetings and later that marketing sourced this quarter. Outbound is not in that goal. It is still on the channel chart.
    • Net revenue retention is this year's ARR for customers who were here a year ago, divided by that older book.
    • AI adoption is the share of reports in the last 30 complete days that used an AI impression. The last two days of the feed are still loading and stay out.
    • Minutes saved takes the mix-adjusted report-time change at Northbay, multiplies by that group's recent studies, and divides by the radiologists who read them.

    Definitions live in dbt/models/semantic.yml. A Snowflake row access policy example sits in dbt/analyses and is not executed on DuckDB.
