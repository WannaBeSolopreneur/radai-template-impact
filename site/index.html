Problem

Northbay Radiology Partners turned on new report templates on May 4, 2026 and asked if radiologists were actually finishing reports faster.

Solution

We compare each modality with itself, then weight those results by the old case mix, so a shift toward more MRI is not read as a change in speed.

Demo

How it's built

The page reads tested marts. A group under 30 studies is marked too few studies and gets no interval. Intervals are a 95% percentile bootstrap with 1,000 resamples and seed 20261103.

  • Median report turnaround is minutes from exam completed to signed.
  • Median time on the report is minutes from started to signed. Templates act here, not on the queue.
  • AI impression acceptance is accepted divided by accepted, edited, and rejected.
  • Template usage is the share of studies that used a template.
  • Words dictated is the median word count.
  • The case mix change compares inside each modality, then weights by the pre-launch mix.

Every customer mart keeps one customer. Test fixtures never land in a mart. The open two days stay out of the metric window.

where customer_id = 'cust_northbay'
  and is_test_fixture = 0
  and is_partial_period = 0

Definitions live in dbt/models/semantic.yml. A Snowflake row access policy example sits in dbt/analyses and is not executed on DuckDB.
